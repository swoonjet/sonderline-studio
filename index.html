At a glance

The opportunity

Social is Sonderline's highest-volume content. Every message ships in four or more formats, and most of the hours go into adapting it, not into ideas.

The system

Seven steps on one source of truth: brief, Claude drafting, deterministic composition, automated checks, human review, delivery and a learning loop.

Quality

13 guide rules checked on every output. A failure blocks approval. New copy routes to product marketing. Designers keep the final craft call.

Video

The Video Studio turns the same rules into short video posts. The photography pans, the Contour lines draw on, the type wipes on and then holds, and cuts land on the beat of original music.

Status

The engines, checks, both Studios, the CLI and Claude drafting all work. Intake, DAM, scheduler and SSO integrations are designed but not built.
