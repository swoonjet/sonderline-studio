5 Approve and export

The PowerPoint file is editable: every line of copy is a text box in Inter Tight (install the font from the brand kit to see it exactly), the logo is the supplied artwork, and speaker notes are included. Colour fields, photos and graphics are a background picture on each slide.

Prototype by Jon Toews for SketchDeck. Sonderline is a fictional brand. The photography supplied with the brand kit is AI-generated. Contour is a proposed flow graphic based on the S symbol. Inter Tight, SIL OFL 1.1. Exports use PptxGenJS (MIT) and jsPDF (MIT).
